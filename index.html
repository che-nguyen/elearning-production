Stage 02 Faster

Content Design

Turning raw subject knowledge into a structured script — and into other languages.

Idea & script + translation
[ image / GIF / video ]drop file named contentdesign-before / contentdesign-after
Before

Ideas came from SMEs and team brainstorm. Translation to other languages used Google Translate.

⚠ Quality, context & terminology not guaranteed
After

Prompt Gemini & ChatGPT to generate and shape ideas, then finalize with the SME. Translate with Gemini for context-aware results.

✓ Faster ideation, SME stays in control of accuracy
Stage 03 Major time saved

Illustration Preparation

Building the on-screen presenter, the voice, and the visuals — the most expertise-heavy stage before AI.

① On-screen narrator
[ image / GIF / video ]narrator-before / narrator-after
Before

Illustrated by hand in Adobe Illustrator, then animated in Premiere.

⚠ Needs deep design + motion-graphic expertise & a high-spec machine
After

Generate a talking AI avatar in HeyGen — no drawing, no manual animation, no high-end hardware.

✓ Minutes instead of days
② Voice-over
[ image / GIF / audio ]voiceover-before / voiceover-after
Before

Recorded EN & VI from an external MC.

⚠ Quality depends on their environment & voice health — hard to keep consistent
After

Generate voice with ElevenLabs & HeyGen — consistent tone and pace, even when dubbing into other languages.

✓ One consistent voice across every language
③ Visuals (footage)
[ image / GIF / video ]visual-before / visual-after
Before

Shot on location in-store. If a shot missed the standard, the whole crew had to re-shoot.

⚠ Re-shoots cost crew, time & location booking
After

Still shoot in-store — but fix and re-edit images/video with AI instead of returning to re-shoot.

✓ Fix in software, skip the second shoot
Stage 04 Major time saved

Digital Development

Editing the video, adding subtitles, and cloning the course into other languages.

Subtitles & multi-language cloning
[ image / GIF / video ]subtitle-before / subtitle-after
Before

Subtitles typed in manually, sentence by sentence. Cloning the course into another language meant starting from the very beginning.

⚠ Every language = a full restart
After

Auto-generate subtitles in CapCut Pro, then clone the course into other languages using the AI features in TalentLMS.

✓ Clone, don't rebuild — new languages in a fraction of the time
What changed for the team

Same output. A shifted skillset.

The final deliverable is still a SCORM package. What changed is the expertise it takes to build one.
